RESEARCH BOUNDARY

Evaluation safeguards

  • Moving averages, volatility and liquidity measures use lagged observations. Signals cannot be executed before the next session’s open.
  • Automatic selection ranks a fixed parameter grid by training-period excess Sharpe, less a turnover penalty. Test-period results do not enter this ranking.
  • Changing settings after inspecting test results introduces multiple-testing bias. Manual mode is exploratory, not independent out-of-sample evidence.
  • CSI 300, 500, 800 and 1000 use Qlib’s historical index membership. The CSI 2000 and microcap universes are proxies based on lagged liquidity, not official index constituents.
  • These results are research records, not investment advice or a promise of future returns.
